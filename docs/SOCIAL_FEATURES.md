# Social & Community Features

RefWiki has evolved into a social officiating platform where fans can discuss and rate calls made during live games.

## Community Feed
The **Community Feed** (available on both Web and Mobile) is a global timeline of "Calls" published by users.

*   **Publishing**: Users can choose to keep their logs private or check "Publish to Community Feed" during the Log Call process.
*   **Visibility**: Public calls show the sport, teams involved, the rule reference, and the user's personal notes.
*   **Privacy**: Usernames in the community feed are anonymized (only the prefix of their email is displayed).

## Voting System
Consensus is built through a live community voting mechanism.

*   **Controversy Scale**: Fans can vote on a scale of 1 to 5:
    1.  **Textbook**: Clear-cut, no debate.
    2.  **Technically Correct**: Letter of the law vs. Spirit of the game.
    3.  **Let 'em Play**: Ticky-tack call.
    4.  **Game Changer**: Massive, high-impact penalty.
    5.  **Total Robbery**: Officials absolutely blew the call.
*   **Integrity**: Each user is limited to one vote per call. Subsequent votes will update their existing rating rather than adding a new one.
*   **Live Averages**: The average rating and total vote count are calculated in real-time by the backend.

## Hall of Shame (Leaderboard)
The **Leaderboard** highlights the most controversial officiating moments.

*   **Algorithm**: Rankings are determined by the highest average community rating.
*   **Minimum Threshold**: To prevent single-vote skewing, calls must typically reach a community-defined threshold of votes before appearing in the top ranks.
*   **Visual Indicators**: The Hall of Shame uses color-coding (Green for clean calls, Red for robberies) to provide an instant heat map of recent officiating quality.
